* Random Ideas

** Potential Scenes

*** Coffee shop at 07:05 versus 07:45
The same room twenty minutes apart is a different room. Alex learns this
absurdly late, and it is the cheapest possible illustration of the theme.

Status: PROMOTED — it is Chapter 4 Scene 1.

*** Alex's work crisis, live
A client video call while Alex is visibly frazzled, to make the professional
stakes concrete rather than reported.

Status: Rejected. The Halloran loss already does this and does it *to* Alex
rather than in front of an audience. Two of these would flatten the escalation
— see the Stakes table in design.org, where quarter I is money and only
quarter I is money.

*** Sam's vulnerability
Sam reads as too perfect. Need Sam to be running something harder than Alex
is, not to be serene.

Status: PROMOTED, and it restructured the book. Sam is not a mentor: the
notebook is a medication log, the routine is a scaffold, and the mentor
reading is Alex's mistake. Everything downstream of that — Bell Lane, the
Thursday call, the moral decision — came out of this note.

*** The watch stops
What if the watch stops? Original idea: Alex panics, then realizes they have
internalized the routine and no longer need the symbol.

Status: PROMOTED, inverted. The self-congratulatory version was too
heavy-handed. It stops because Alex never wound it — the neglect is the point
— and the recovery is winding it, not transcending it. See design.org,
Symbols.

** Interesting Dialogues

*** Alex, internal
"Five more minutes wouldn't hurt." — the repeated lie, and it should appear
exactly twice: Chapter 1 and, in a different context, Chapter 6.

"Close enough." — about the socks, about the invoice, about the hour.

*** Sam
"I used to be exactly like you." — the key line. Alex repeats it verbatim in
the last scene, which is the only signal the ending gets that the wheel keeps
turning.

"Build it around the thing that can't move." — Sam describing their own life
without saying so.

*** Dana
"You do your best work at two in the morning. That's why I call you." — a
compliment, a purchase, and an attack on the Weakness, all in one line.

"I have never missed a deadline." — the credential.

*** Casey
"It's fine, I wasn't doing anything." — reflexive, never true, and Alex's own
Stance 1 in a new mouth.

* Problems to Solve

** Detected plot holes

- Bell Lane geography. If it is twenty-five minutes away, can Sam be at the
  flat at eight *and* at Bell Lane later that morning? Flagged in novel.org,
  Chapter 6 Scene 1, with an *EDIT*: plot marker. Resolve in
  objects/worldbuilding.org before drafting, not during.

** Inconsistencies

- The watch is described as ticking in Chapter 2 Scene 1 and as having an
  alarm in Chapter 3 Scene 1. Both contradict the design and both are already
  flagged. Run =F8 F8 6= and read the =plot= category.

** Unanswered questions

- Is Casey named on the page in the final scene, or left as "a young person"?
  Naming them is warmer and slightly smaller. Flagged with a *NOTE* marker at
  the end of novel.org.
- Does Dana get one moment of the reader's sympathy, or none? The Stance has
  to be arguable either way, but sympathy and arguability are not the same
  thing.

* Creative Decisions

** Point of view

- Narrator :: Third person limited, single focalizer (Alex).
- Tense :: Past.
- Consequence :: Priya's betrayal cannot be dramatized, only evidenced. This
  is a constraint the design took on deliberately; see design.org, "What this
  choice forbids me".

** Tone

Dry and counting. Alex narrates in minutes, and the arithmetic disappears
from the prose in the last scene without comment. That disappearance is the
"Running late" thread closing (it carries =:Weight: -1= in plot.org: tracked,
no column on the board).

** Style

Short scenes. Enter late, leave early. The book is 80,000 words and no scene
is over 1,200.

* Words/Phrases to Avoid

Filler and tics to grep for in Pass 8:

- "somehow"
- "just"
- "seemed to"
- "and Alex understood/realized/knew" — the RUE list; see revision.org,
  Pass 8.

* World Rules

Contemporary realism, so the rules are social rather than physical, and they
live in objects/worldbuilding.org. The three that constrain the plot:

- An hour missed at Bell Lane goes to the next name on the list.
- A retainer at Okonkwo & Field is granted by one person and never
  negotiated.
- The watch runs about thirty-six hours and does not warn you.
