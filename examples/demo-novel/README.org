* What this project is

A complete org-scribe novel project, filled in, so that you can read a worked
example instead of a blank template. It is what =M-x org-scribe-create-novel-project=
produces, three weeks and five scenes later.

Nothing here needs installing. Open any file and read it; open it inside
Emacs with org-scribe loaded and every command in the tables below works
against it.

** Where to start

1. [[file:design.org][design.org]] — the Starting Gate and the Core. Read this first, because the
   book did.
2. [[file:objects/plot.org][objects/plot.org]] — the thirteen non-negotiables, filled in, and the four
   narrative lines.
3. [[file:novel.org][novel.org]] — the manuscript. Five scenes drafted, ten carrying planning
   metadata only.
4. Then =F8 F8 H= (=org-scribe-project-health=) with any of these files open,
   and read what the tool makes of it.

** Synopsis

Alex Rivera has never once been where they said they would be, and has never
once meant to let anyone down. In a city that pays for availability rather
than time, that is a marketable trait — and the two people who benefit from
it most are the client who needs Alex reachable at any hour and the neighbour
who has quietly been covering the one hour Alex keeps spending.

Theme: /if you refuse to give your days a shape, you hand their shape to
whoever asks for it first./

** Deliberate imperfections

The demo is a project mid-draft, not a finished one, so several things are
wrong on purpose and the tools are meant to find them:

- Starting Gate box 7 is unticked although all thirteen plot points are
  written. =F8 F8 H= reports the disagreement and does not resolve it.
- The watch is described as ticking in one scene and as having an alarm in
  another; both contradict [[file:design.org][design.org]]. Both are flagged with =*EDIT*: plot=
  markers and appear in the edit index.
- One Cast Check box is unticked, with the offending scenes flagged
  =*EDIT*: character=.
- Several of the thirteen plot points are served by no scene yet. The health
  report lists them.
