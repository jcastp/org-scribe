# A worked example. Four settings, each with the four Aspects the method
# asks for: an Echo (how the place reflects the Theme), a Hazard (how it
# makes the World Problem worse), a Character Aspect it activates, and a
# Symbol.
#
# A setting with no Aspect at all is scenery, and Pass 5 of the revision
# cuts it. That is why the fifth location this project used to carry — a
# generic mid-sized city with a climate and a population figure — is gone:
# nothing in it reflected the theme, pressed on anybody, or appeared in a
# scene.
#
# The world as a whole lives in worldbuilding.org. This file holds the
# specific places where scenes happen.
#
# Try C-c C-x C-c for column view, and F8 F8 4 to find every scene set in
# one of these.

* Alex's Flat, 4A
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: loc-alex-flat-001
:Type: Setting
:Echo: A day with no edges in it anywhere
:First-appearance: Chapter 1, Scene 1
:END:

- Echo :: Nothing in this flat marks the difference between one hour and the
  next. The blinds are the same shut at seven as at noon, the desk is four
  steps from the bed, and there is no room you have to be in to do a
  particular thing.
- Hazard :: Work is always within reach, so being available costs nothing
  and therefore is never refused.
- Character :: "I do my best work at two in the morning" — the flat is the
  only place where that is comfortably true, which is how it stays true.
- Symbol :: The bed, unmade. In Act I it is absurd that anyone would make it.
  By Act III Alex makes it, and it is the same act.

** Description

One room and a half. A desk pushed against the window and a bed against the
wall behind it, so that turning the chair around is the entire commute. Mail
accumulates on the coffee table in a slope that has its own geology; the
watch is under it for the whole first quarter. The kettle is the loudest
object in the flat and the only thing that reliably marks an hour.

** Scenes that happen here

Linked automatically from each scene's :Location: property (F8 F8 l).
Currently: Chapter 1 Scene 1, Chapter 2 Scene 1, Chapter 3 Scene 1,
Chapter 4 Scene 2, Chapter 7 Scene 1.

* The Corner Coffee Shop
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: loc-coffee-shop-001
:Type: Setting
:Echo: Everyone here is on their way to being somewhere at a time
:First-appearance: Chapter 1, Scene 2
:END:

- Echo :: The only room in the book where the whole city is visibly
  operating on stated hours, and Alex is the one person in the queue who
  does not know what time they have to be anywhere.
- Hazard :: The queue is a place where a phone comes out. Every morning
  message Alex answers in this book is answered standing in this line.
- Character :: Sam's "I've learned to spot the signs" — the observing,
  slightly proud kindness that only works in a room full of strangers.
- Symbol :: The order called out by name. Somebody says your name at
  approximately the time they said they would, and it is the most reliable
  transaction in Alex's day.

** Description

Narrow, loud, and organized to within an inch of its life behind the
counter. Three people deep at 7:45 and empty at 7:05, a fact Alex learns
much later than they should. Two tables, both always taken by people who got
there earlier.

** Scenes that happen here

Chapter 1 Scene 2, Chapter 4 Scene 1, Chapter 8 Scene 2.

* Okonkwo & Field
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: loc-agency-001
:Type: Setting
:Echo: A place where being available is the visible currency
:First-appearance: Chapter 2, Scene 2
:END:

- Echo :: Nobody here has a fixed hour, and everybody is always there. The
  studio proves the theme's negative: hand your shape to whoever asks first
  and you never leave.
- Hazard :: The World Problem in its purest form — a room that pays for
  reachability. Any scene set here can be complicated simply by someone
  answering a message.
- Character :: Dana's "I have never missed a deadline", which is a
  credential in this building and nowhere else.
- Symbol :: The lobby clock, which is four minutes fast and has been for
  years because Dana likes it that way.

** Description

Fourth floor, one long room, and a lobby with a lift that takes ninety
seconds. Desks with no names on them. The clock above the reception desk is
analogue, deliberately, and wrong, deliberately.

** Scenes that happen here

Chapter 2 Scene 2, Chapter 5 Scene 1, Chapter 8 Scene 1 — the battle, which
happens in the lobby and not upstairs, because eleven minutes is the whole
scene.

* Bell Lane Day Centre
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: loc-bell-lane-001
:Type: Setting
:Echo: The one institution in the book that keeps its own hours
:First-appearance: Chapter 6, Scene 1
:END:

- Echo :: Bell Lane calls at 8:00 on Thursdays and will not call at 8:10.
  It is the only thing in the story with a shape that does not bend to
  anyone's availability, and that rigidity is the argument for the theme.
- Hazard :: A waiting list. Any hour missed here is not rescheduled, it is
  reallocated to a name further down.
- Character :: Sam's refusal to ask for anything, which the intake process
  is not designed to accommodate.
- Symbol :: The list itself, on a clipboard, with names on it.

** Description

A converted primary school with the height markings still on one doorframe.
Bright, understaffed, and organized around a timetable pinned by the door
that everybody reads and nobody questions.

** Scenes that happen here

Chapter 6 Scene 1, Chapter 8 Scene 1 (the destination, twenty-five minutes
from the lobby).
